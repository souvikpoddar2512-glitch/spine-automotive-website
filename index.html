Company Background

Built with Heavy Engineering at Our Core

Established in 2025 in the industrial heartland of Seraikela-Kharsawan and Jamshedpur, Spine Automotive Private Limited delivers structural integrity and dependable fleet solutions.

Engineered for Strength. Fabricated for Longevity.

Spine Automotive Private Limited is dedicated to the manufacturing of commercial motor vehicles, trailers, and semi-trailers designed to handle the toughest freight routes across India.

Operating from NS-39, Phase-1, Adityapur Industrial Area, our facility benefits directly from being inside Asia's largest auto-ancillary and heavy industrial belt. With close proximity to Tata Steel and India's prime steel producers, we have immediate access to high-yield structural steel, specialized metallurgy, and seasoned automotive fabrication talent.

  • Our Mission: To manufacture robust, payload-optimized commercial trailers and vehicle bodies that minimize downtime and enhance fleet profitability.
  • Our Vision: To grow into a trusted OEM manufacturing partner recognized across Eastern India for engineering rigor, honesty, and build precision.
  • The Adityapur Advantage: Direct integration with the regional industrial supply ecosystem ensures consistent material quality and agile build cycles.

Engineering & Production

Manufacturing Capabilities

From raw plate preparation to final paint and dimensional inspection, our Adityapur facility applies rigorous production processes to ensure every trailer meets high durability benchmarks.

01

Raw Material & CNC Profiling

Procurement of certified high-tensile structural steel directly from regional steel mills. Plates are CNC plasma-cut with high edge accuracy and dimensional consistency.

02

Jig Fixturing & Heavy Welding

Precision chassis jigs ensure zero chassis twist or alignment deviation. Longitudinal beams are welded with high-penetration submerged arc and multi-pass MIG welding.

03

Surface Prep & Protective Coating

Shot blasting and abrasive mechanical surface preparation eliminate scale and oxidation. High-build anti-corrosive epoxy primers followed by premium industrial polyurethane topcoats.

04

Inspection & Quality Audit

Laser alignment of axles, ultrasonic/dye penetrant weld verification, brake line pressure testing, and full dimensional checks prior to client dispatch.
